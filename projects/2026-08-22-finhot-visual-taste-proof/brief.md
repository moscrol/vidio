# FinHot visual-taste proof brief

## Objective

Prove whether `vibe-visual-taste` can turn the same FinHot content and real product evidence into a clearer, more ownable static frame without relying on a named-brand imitation or a larger asset budget.

## Upstream language gate

Before visual-contract review, `reference-brief.md` records the exact supplied, corpus-audit, design-language, and component-directory evidence with explicit borrow/exclude boundaries. `design-vocabulary.json` fixes five project terms: `TERM-001` evidence rail, `TERM-002` content dominance, `TERM-003` accent scarcity, `TERM-004` literal product proof, and `TERM-005` editorial restraint.

The pair translates the three requested aesthetic phrases into observable checks and passes the language linter at 0 errors and 0 warnings. Each variant's `DESIGN.md` and QC consume the canonical IDs, while static numeric choices remain owned by each `visual-contract.json` without redefining the vocabulary. This proof defines no motion decision.

## Locked content

Both variants must contain the following exact content:

- Kicker: `FINHOT / SIGNAL WORKSPACE`
- Headline: `把一整天的公开信源，压成可判断的研究流`
- Evidence 01: `多源聚合：雪球、微博、公众号与更多公开信源`
- Evidence 02: `质量排序：高信号内容先浮上来`
- Evidence 03: `原文可追：保留来源，不替你下结论`
- Product label: `真实界面 / 全部动态`
- Source: `FinHot 产品界面（本地截图，2026-08-22）`
- Disclaimer: `演示仅说明信息整理流程，不构成投资建议。内容来自公开信源，最终判断由用户完成。`
- Media: the same `assets/d-feed.png` file.

## Controlled variable

Only the static visual system changes: hierarchy, typography roles, palette roles, geometry, depth mechanism, surface effects, and composition. Copy, source image, canvas, and factual meaning remain fixed. There is no motion or audio.

## Acceptance

- Both HTML frames render at exactly 1080 × 1920 with no scrollbar or runtime error.
- The project-level `reference-brief.md` and `design-vocabulary.json` pass the design-language gate with zero errors and zero warnings.
- Both visual contracts have zero hard errors.
- Baseline warnings match the deliberately encoded anti-patterns and are documented.
- Distilled contract has zero errors and zero warnings.
- The distilled frame has one obvious focal claim at 1× and 25% size, survives grayscale, keeps required content inside safe area, and preserves readable source/disclaimer text.
- The final 2-up evidence board is generated from the final captured frames.
- QC ends with `APPROVE` or `BLOCK`.

This gate does not alter the locked copy, shared source-image bytes, HTML frames, rendered pixels, or historical comparison evidence.
