# FinHot motion-taste proof

This proof isolates the value of Vidio's self-owned motion-taste layer.

- Format: 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 8 seconds, silent.
- Content lock: both variants use the same FinHot screenshots, copy, layout, and palette.
- Baseline: preserves the legacy motion character—concurrent focal beats, bounce-first chips, moving-camera copy, and a hard scene cut.
- Distilled: applies one-focus-at-a-time sequencing, restrained easing, a causal feed-to-rank bridge, camera settle, and a readable final hold.
- Gate: both contracts must have zero hard errors; the distilled contract must have zero warnings. Both compositions must pass HyperFrames `check`, render, and visual frame review.

The proof is intentionally silent and short. It measures motion hierarchy, not audio design or a broader visual redesign.
